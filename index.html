Why we built it

You care. Your calendar just doesn't.

Almost every adult we interviewed feels guilty about not reaching out enough, and they blame busy schedules, not a lack of caring. Meanwhile, phones and apps are hard for many older relatives, so staying connected falls on one person.

The guilt is real

Work and family fill the day, and "I'll call Mom this weekend" turns into another week.

Tech gets in the way

Many grandparents don't text or navigate menus, so group chats and photo apps leave them out.

One person carries it

One sibling usually ends up doing the calls, the appointments and the remembering for everyone.

Everything in one place

One app instead of five.

Generations handles the tedious parts of staying in touch, so the time you do have goes to the people you love.

Privacy and trust

Your family's moments stay in your family.

Generations makes money only from subscriptions. We never show ads and never sell your photos or data.

Private by default

Posts, photos, calendars and people are visible only to your family's members. Our server rules enforce that, not just the app.

Join by code only

New members can join only with your family's invite code, and photos are stored in your family's own secure folder.

Dignity for your loved one

No location tracking and no monitoring. Generations keeps seniors included and independent, not watched.

Built from real conversations

Every feature answers something families told us.

We interviewed adults who care for or keep in touch with aging relatives. Here's what we heard, and what we built in response.
